Full policy

This policy describes how AI-LinguaKit may collect, use, and protect information when you use the application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1Information we collect +

AI-LinguaKit may collect or access the following categories of information:

  • Text you enter for translation, text-to-speech, or language identification.
  • Images you choose to scan when using OCR-related features.
  • Downloaded language pack status and local translation history stored on your device.
  • Technical device and app information required by Google Play services, Google AdMob, and billing flows.
  • Permission state information, such as whether camera or audio access has been granted.
2How information is used +
  • To translate text, identify languages, recognize text from images, and support text-to-speech features.
  • To maintain translation history and downloaded model availability within the app.
  • To unlock premium features, reward-based benefits, and purchase-linked functionality.
  • To support ad delivery, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and service reliability where third-party ad services are used.
3Ads and monetization +

AI-LinguaKit uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads in supported parts of the app. AdMob may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location signals, app interaction events, and ad performance data according to Google’s own privacy terms.

Rewarded ads may also be used to grant temporary in-app benefits like extra usage credits or limited feature unlocks.

4Purchases and billing +

Purchases, subscriptions, and entitlement validation are handled through Google Play Billing. AI-LinguaKit does not directly process your card details. Payment information is handled by Google under Google Play’s billing and payments policies.

5Permissions +
  • Camera: used to capture or scan images for OCR and text extraction.
  • Microphone / audio-related access: used where speech input or speech features require it.
  • Network access: used for model downloads, ads, billing, and related Google services.
  • Media/image access: used when you choose images from your device.
6Data storage and security +

AI-LinguaKit stores certain app data locally on your device, including translation history, downloaded pack information, and app preferences. While reasonable efforts are made to protect local and service-linked data, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7Your choices +
  • You can revoke app permissions through Android system settings.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.
  • You can manage subscriptions and purchase settings through Google Play.
  • You can review Google’s ad privacy and billing terms for service-specific controls.
8Children’s privacy +

AI-LinguaKit is not intentionally directed toward collecting personal information from children. If you believe information from a child has been provided inappropriately, contact the app publisher so the issue can be reviewed.

9Policy updates +

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, or third-party service updates. The latest published version on this page should be treated as the current version.

10Contact +

For privacy questions related to AI-LinguaKit, please contact the app publisher through the support or contact information associated with the app listing or repository.

This page is provided as a human-readable summary and formal app policy for AI-LinguaKit. If store disclosures, SDK terms, or legal requirements change, this policy should be updated accordingly.
